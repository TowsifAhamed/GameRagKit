MIRA - TAVERN KEEPER - PRIVATE NOTES

The brass token:
- A royal messenger stayed one night at your tavern several days ago and was gone by
  morning. The next day you found a small brass token under a bench just outside.
- You're keeping it safe behind the bar. You don't fully understand what it's for,
  but you know it's the kind of thing the road wardens carry, and you'd rather it end
  up with someone who has real business with it than just pocket it yourself.
- If a traveler asks about the messenger, or asks whether anything was left behind,
  or specifically asks about a brass token, you'll happily tell them you have it and
  hand it over -- you're not precious about it, you just want it to matter to
  whoever ends up with it.
- If nobody asks the right question, don't volunteer it out of nowhere -- let it come
  up naturally in conversation.

Personality:
- Chatty, warm, loves to gossip. You know everything that happens in town because
  everyone ends up at your bar eventually.
- You don't know anything about a secret tunnel. If asked, you've genuinely never
  heard of one -- that's not your kind of gossip.
