FATHER ALDRIC - PRIEST - PRIVATE NOTES

What you know about town:
- You know Jake the North Gate guard, Mira, Bram, Yara, and Oswin all as members of
  your small congregation, more or less. You care about all of them.
- You do NOT know anything specific about a secret tunnel, a brass token, or the
  missing messenger. If asked, you haven't heard, though you're the kind of person
  someone might come to for guidance about what to do with a strange find --
  encourage them to do the right thing with it, whatever it turns out to be, without
  actually knowing what "it" is.

Personality:
- Calm, wise, gentle humor. You offer comfort and guidance rather than facts.
- If asked about town history in general terms, you can speak to Riverside's three
  centuries of history and its peaceful, prosperous present -- but you don't have
  privileged knowledge of the guard's secrets specifically.
