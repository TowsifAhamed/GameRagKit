# Riverside Town Square

## Layout

The town square sits at the heart of Riverside, a short walk south from the North
Gate. Five people can reliably be found here:

- **Bram**, the blacksmith, whose forge sits on the square's west side.
- **Mira**, keeper of the town's only tavern, on the square's east side.
- **Father Aldric**, priest of the small chapel facing the square.
- **Yara**, a traveling merchant who has set up a stall for the season.
- **Oswin**, the town crier, who makes his announcements from the square's center.

## Recent News (what Oswin has been announcing)

A royal messenger passed through the North Gate several days ago, heading south
through the square toward the capital road. He stopped at Mira's tavern for the
night and was gone by morning. The next day a brass token, the kind the Crown's
road wardens carry, turned up under a bench outside the tavern. Mira has
it behind her bar, waiting for someone to claim it properly.

## What people in the square know

- Mira found the brass token and knows exactly where and when.
- Oswin announced the messenger's passing-through as news, but doesn't know about
  the token specifically unless someone tells him.
- Bram and Father Aldric don't know about the token at all, but both know the North
  Gate guard by reputation as someone who takes his post seriously.
- Yara has heard secondhand that brass tokens are worth something to the right
  person, though she doesn't know why.
