# Riverside

## Overview

Riverside is a walled trading town on the northern road, built where the old river
crossing meets the king's highway. It has stood for three centuries, growing rich on
trade passing between the northern hill country and the capital.

## The North Gate

The North Gate is the only entrance from the northern road. It is watched day and
night by a single guard, currently Jake, who answers to the Crown's road wardens
rather than the town's own militia. Anyone entering from the north passes through
here first, before reaching the town square.

## The Secret Tunnel

Long ago, during a period of unrest, the town's founders built a hidden tunnel from
the North Gate guardhouse to a cellar beneath the town square, so a warden could
move between the two without being seen from the street. Knowledge of the tunnel was
never made public. It is passed down only to the North Gate guard, and only
confirmed to travelers who carry a brass token, a small stamped disc issued by the
Crown's road wardens to messengers and other trusted travelers.

Almost no brass tokens remain in circulation. The last one anyone in town has seen
was carried by a royal messenger who passed through several days ago and has not
been seen since.

## Current Era

The town is at peace. Trade is steady, the market is busy, and the biggest concern
on anyone's mind is the missing messenger and whatever became of his token.
